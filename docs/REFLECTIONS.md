At first I thought the script wasn't working because I pressed F12 inside VS Code instead of the browser.

I also learned that file paths are different from filenames.

---

Sprint 002 taught me JavaScript, and at first everything about it confused me. Variables, the DOM, all of it.

It didn't click from reading about it. It clicked from doing it, then reading it over a few times until it made sense.

The moment it actually landed was going to the actual page and watching it happen. Clicking a chamber and seeing the active state move to it for real, not just printed in the Console.

Something else happened this sprint that had nothing to do with code. I found out my whole project had been living inside OneDrive the entire time, and I had never once thought to check where my own files actually lived.

What I'm taking from this sprint is that understanding doesn't come from the explanation. It comes from doing something enough times that the explanation starts to make sense afterward.
