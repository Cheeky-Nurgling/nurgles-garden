# 📖 Lore of Nurgle's Garden

> *"Every fortress has a history. Every history begins with a single stone."*

---

## The First Growth

The Little Lord stood before an empty garden with only a single seed in hand.

Others saw barren soil.

The Little Lord saw possibility.

The first stone was laid.

The first page was written.

The Garden began to remember.

---

## The Garden Awakens

The Fortress stood complete.

Its halls echoed with silence.

Its walls remembered every stone that had been placed.

Yet the Garden could not answer.

The Little Lord searched the ancient library.

There, hidden among forgotten records, he discovered a language not spoken by kings, soldiers, or scribes.

It was a language written for the Garden itself.

A chamber was prepared.

A path was opened.

The first words were placed within it.

For a moment, nothing appeared to change.

The Fortress remained still.

Then the Little Lord opened the proper window and listened again.

From beyond the visible walls came the first response:

🌿 The Garden awakens...

The Fortress had found its voice.

The Long Vigil had entered a new age.

## The Garden Learns to Listen

The Fortress had found its voice.

Yet it heard nothing in return.

Six chambers lined its halls.

Visitors could walk to any of them.

They could touch any door.

Still, only one lamp burned.

It burned in the Sanctuary, and it never moved.

The Little Lord returned to the ancient library.

He studied the records he had already learned to read.

He learned how to name every chamber the Fortress held.

He learned how to visit each door in turn.

He learned how to wait until a hand touched one.

The words were written.

```javascript
const allLinks = document.querySelectorAll(".navigation-link");

for (const link of allLinks) {
  link.addEventListener("click", function () {
    for (const otherLink of allLinks) {
      otherLink.classList.remove("active");
    }
    link.classList.add("active");
  });
}
```

For a moment, the halls were quiet.

Then the Little Lord touched the door of the Forge.

The lamp of the Sanctuary faded.

The lamp of the Forge caught.

He touched the Archives, and the flame followed.

He touched the Greenhouse, the Reliquary, and the Apothecary.

Each time, one flame burned.

Each time, it followed his hand.

Nothing was lost in the passing.

The old flame gave itself to the new.

The Fortress had found its voice.

Now it had learned to listen.

The Long Vigil was no longer a solitary one.
